## Key Result

> Historical spatial seismicity rates provide the strongest validated
> probabilistic forecasting baseline for the available Bangladesh earthquake
> catalog. Under strict chronological, spatial, and spatiotemporal validation,
> the tested ETAS and machine-learning formulations do not demonstrate
> statistically defensible incremental predictive skill beyond this baseline.

## What this does NOT claim

- Does NOT claim earthquakes cannot be predicted
- Does NOT claim Bangladesh has no earthquake triggering
- Does NOT claim ETAS proves there are no aftershocks
- Does NOT claim ML is useless
- Does NOT claim Coulomb does not work
- Does NOT present a precise M≥7 probability
